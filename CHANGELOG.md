# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Added an accessibility screen (Options > QFS Accessibility, or Mods > Config) with an on/off switch for each option.
- Cyan Blood (the ectoplasm blood) is now an option (on by default). The blood, flesh and shroomlight textures, the cyan particles and the Visceral Heap fog colour all follow it.
- Added Vanilla Mob Skins: the QFSExtra Cucurucho and Dark Cucurucho mobs use vanilla skins (from the QFS Vanilla Mob Skins pack 1.1.1).
- Added Friendly Spiders: every spider mob, vanilla or modded, shows as a slime with purple sparkles, cat meows and matching captions.
- Added Friendly Bugs: silverfish, endermites and bugs from Alex's Mobs and Alex's Caves show as small slimes with slime sounds. The list and the replacement mob are in the config.
- Added Hole-Free Textures: sponges, honeycomb, barnacles, porous blocks and similar textures lose their holes and bubbles.
- Added the Loud Sound Limiter: mob and weather sounds turn down when a loud sound starts and rise back over about three seconds, without touching the volume sliders.
- The mod's assets moved into built-in resource packs under `resourcepacks/`, combined into one pack named QFS Accessibility.
- The accessibility screen is translated into 16 languages.
- Options are saved in `config/bop_spectre_moon-client.toml`.
